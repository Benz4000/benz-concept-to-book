# Synthetic goal and outcome audit

Private goal-audit records have been replaced with this generic example. The table illustrates how to consolidate repeated work without claiming a real project's history.

| Goal | Evidence to examine | Sufficient result | Reopen when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earned character change | Decisions, costs, relationship tests and ending | Conduct demonstrates the intended arc | Relevant canon or ending changes |
| Consistent scene depth | Representative scenes with action, resistance and aftermath | Scenes meet the project's benchmark without padding | Actual thinness or new direction appears |
| Original, readable voice | Accepted examples and actual prose | Agreed voice criteria are met | Audience or voice direction changes |
| Accurate reader package | Section inventory, chronology and knowledge limits | Included sections serve their purpose and placement | Canon, inventory or disclosure changes |
| Clear visual identity | Current briefs, appearance references and actual art/type | Required surfaces are coherent and legible | Relevant assets, direction or geometry changes |
| Reproducible deliverable | Exact inputs, configuration, outputs and real checks | Requested files and evidence are complete | Inputs or requirements change |

Before another pass: identify its goal, demonstrated gap, authoritative inputs, protected qualities, affected scope, required reference and completion check. Combine compatible issues; retain valid unrelated evidence. A new requirement is a scoped foundation change. A resolved measurement completes its dependent work. A repair corrects a demonstrated failure against an established criterion.

Source recommendations and inferred preferences remain candidates until supported for their intended scope. Actual source consultations and changes belong in the project's source ledger. No private vault or earlier skill is required. See [the skill](../SKILL.md).
