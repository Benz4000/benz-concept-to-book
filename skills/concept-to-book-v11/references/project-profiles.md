# Project profiles

Use profiles to select relevant work, then narrow it to the requested endpoint. Profiles are composable descriptions of this project, not genre formulas or sources of creative preferences. Recover accepted choices before adding fields. Merge the selection into the existing [foundation](../assets/templates/foundation.md); do not create a profile dossier.

## Select the applicable combination

Choose only the dimensions that affect upcoming work. An original, standalone, text-only outline needs fewer records than an illustrated adaptation in a series. A book may contain both original and adapted material, or have illustrated and text-only editions. Mark the applicable units and editions rather than forcing one label onto everything.

| Profile or overlay | Establish when relevant | Work it activates | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original material | Reader promise, original contribution, working canon, factual research boundaries, accepted voice and scope | Develop the premise and arcs; track consequential invented rules and research uses | No requirement for source fiction, a mythology, real-world setting, or invented citations; original fiction may still need factual research |
| Adaptation | Actual source work/edition/translation and read scope; fidelity and departure boundaries; what is retained, combined, invented, or uncertain | Map consequential source choices to story/companion/art destinations; use [source conflicts](source-conflicts.md) when accounts differ | No presumed definitive version, universal fidelity rule, full-source reading claim, or automatic permission to reuse protected material |
| Series | Existing canon and accepted decisions by series/book/edition; chronology, recurring identity, reader knowledge and promised cross-book payoffs | Maintain only useful shared continuity and book-specific exceptions; recheck affected books when shared facts change | No automatic equal chapter counts, matched lengths, required recaps, fixed cover layouts, or permission to rewrite other books |
| Standalone | This book's promise, necessary context, ending and supporting sections | Verify the book supplies its intended context and pays off its own promises | No series bible, future-volume plan, sequels, or compulsory companion material |
| Illustrated interior | Intended image roles and coverage, stable scene/appearance evidence, protected text/art, and requested edition behavior | Brief and integrate planned assets; track versions/placement; inspect descriptions, reading order and image relationships where applicable | No art in every chapter, mandatory panoramas/portraits, fixed density, new asset commission, or conversion of every format into an illustrated edition |
| Text-only interior | Intended narrative and reader sections; any intentional exceptions such as maps or diagrams | Complete prose and navigation checks; omit unnecessary interior-art tasks | No need for empty image manifests; no prohibition on separately requested cover work or an existing meaningful diagram |

Profiles activate questions and checks, not answers. Tone, viewpoint, cultural treatment, art style, trim, chapter counts, whitespace, balance, typography, colors and publishing service remain scoped user choices or documented working assumptions. Case-study examples do not supply defaults. Resolve overlapping profiles at the affected unit: a source-derived prologue can require adaptation decisions while the rest of an original novel does not.

## Limit work by endpoint

| Requested endpoint | Relevant completion evidence | Defer unless needed now |
|---|---|---|
| Concept or outline | Coherent intended story/architecture, required constraints, relevant source decisions, consequential continuity and open questions | Full drafting, generated art, typesetting, ISBNs, printer specifications, release packages |
| Manuscript or revision | Requested narrative and supporting sections, source/continuity evidence, actual editorial reading coverage and resolved required defects | Final edition geometry, print/export tests and covers outside the request |
| Illustrated or reading proof | Named representative/full coverage, integrated requested material, actual artifact and format checks, disclosed provisional content | Untested remainder, other editions, physical manufacture or platform acceptance |
| Publication files | Actual requested exports, exact inputs/outputs, relevant rendered and format checks, unresolved destination-specific requirements | Upload, purchase, submission, proof approval and distribution without authority |
| Authorized external action | Exact approved scope and file pair; evidenced resulting state | Later publishing states or other actions not included in the authorization |

A profile never grants external-action authority. Do not contact readers, obtain credentials, commission assets, upload files, or create accounts simply because the profile could eventually use them. Obtain current destination requirements and credentials only when relevant to an authorized step; missing publishing information does not block independent writing.

## Record a compact selection

Use these fields in the foundation only where useful:

- Active profiles and scope: book, sections, series overlay, and edition exceptions.
- Requested endpoint and authoritative current material.
- Activated work and why it matters now; omitted work and any consequential deferral.
- Accepted creative choices, their evidence and scope; unresolved assumptions separately.
- Representative proof or review needed for an uncertain interaction, or valid evidence already available.
- Reopen trigger: a changed source, shared canon, requested content type, format, or endpoint.

For example, an original series manuscript with a text-only interior can need recurring-character knowledge checks without an art workflow or a print proof. An illustrated adaptation outline can establish image roles and source departures without generating images. These examples establish no preferences for another project.

At a scope change, update the affected selection and dependencies; preserve unrelated valid work. Use [continuity and knowledge](continuity-and-knowledge.md), [project records](project-records.md), [format reading checks](format-reading-checks.md), and [outcome stages](outcome-stages.md) only for the relevant work.
