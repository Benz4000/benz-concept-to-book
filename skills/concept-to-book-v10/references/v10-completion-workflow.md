# Complete the requested book and handoff

V10 retains the [fifteen-step dependency order](workflow-order.md), [foundation](foundation-settings.md), A–L responsibilities and complete [CD01–CD12 cover workflow](cover-design-workflow.md). The six enhancements below close specific production gaps within that route. They do not require a new outline, additional literary passes, or every output format.

## 1. Whole-book review across bounded batches

Use the [cover/render guide](cover-assembly-and-rendered-review.md) when required visual coverage spans multiple render packets. Retain the exact PDF, packet and preview identities plus actual page dispositions. Aggregate records only after validating their shared document identity and coverage; disclose missing pages and conflicts. A tool can verify records and byte identities, not who truly inspected a page or how carefully.

Keep selected-page acceptance separate from full-document coverage. An observation about page flow cannot establish literary review, accessibility or printer acceptance. A revised PDF needs current evidence; unchanged page text alone does not prove unchanged graphics. Resolve conflicting review findings explicitly rather than letting the latest filename win.

## 2. Paragraph and image-caption pagination

Establish relevant split-line and image/caption grouping preferences in the foundation. Apply the supported controls from the [print guide](print-and-export-checks.md), preserving the original text and supplied fonts. Keep legacy/default behavior visible; do not silently turn defaults into a global author preference.

Use representative short and long paragraphs, mixed formatting, long headings and demanding images/captions to test feasibility before scaling. Inspect actual page boundaries and neighboring pages after a repair. If the requested group cannot fit its frame, report the specific conflict and use a supported authorized layout adjustment. Do not shorten prose, shrink type, add illustrations or alter trim solely to hide a failure.

## 3. Reader links and source notes

Use the documented [import subset](manuscript-import.md) and [production contract](production-adapters.md). Establish which citations belong in the reader edition, note placement/treatment, identity and link-label conventions. Preserve reference identities, intended destinations, meaningful formatting and supported return navigation through actual exports. Verify source assertions editorially; link integrity alone does not establish correct citation or factual support.

Reader-facing notes and the internal [source ledger](reference-provenance.md) serve different purposes. Do not publish private research notes or turn every consulted craft guide into an in-book citation. A note ID may link to an existing source record, but file/excerpt identity, reading scope, interpretation and application remain distinct evidence. Unsupported notes, links or document structures require another suitable adapter or an explicit bounded conversion; never silently delete them.

## 4. Digital validation and scoped reader evidence

Use [format-reading checks](format-reading-checks.md) to run an explicitly configured available validator against the exact EPUB and retain real tool/version/report evidence. Preserve warnings and errors. Missing tools, timeouts, malformed results and unperformed checks are visible states, not passes. A successful local build and an actual external validator report establish different coverage.

Record reader observations separately: exact file, viewer/version or environment when known, conditions, scope, method, observation and remaining limits. Relevant checks include navigation, reflow at different sizes, image descriptions and knowledge boundaries. An explicit test record does not prove the test happened; do not invent observations to fill the schema. No automatic accessibility, cross-reader compatibility or physical acceptance follows from the validator.

## 5. Barcode content and edition identity

At CD09–CD11, follow [barcode verification](barcode-verification.md) to compare supported decoded values from the supplied barcode and the actual rendered cover region with the explicit edition identifier. Verify actual inputs, not a filename or another edition's metadata. Keep source and rendered results separate so placement damage cannot hide behind a good source image.

Unreadable, unsupported, ambiguous or mismatched data remain unresolved or failed according to the check. A correct check digit or decoded value does not establish identifier ownership, assignment, copyright, physical scanner performance or printer acceptance. Obtain the edition identifier from a reliable project source; never invent an assigned ISBN. Preserve original barcode/artwork and prior covers during repair.

## 6. Connected local production and handoff

Use the optional [runner](project-runner-and-source-report.md) to coordinate selected stages and their dependencies. A plan exposes inputs, capabilities and proposed work; execution uses explicit choices and new output locations. Cover, render, source, review and package stages should consume the exact selected products, not nearby files that happen to look final.

On resume, verify consumed inputs, helper/runtime/configuration and complete outputs before reuse. Missing, changed or incomplete work requires renewed dependent stages. Retain prior runs and unrelated valid evidence; do not recreate history by copying an old review status onto new bytes. A different edition can retain current evidence only when its relevant inputs and requirements remain unchanged.

Handoff packages may be pending candidates. Label their actual state and carry source reports, review coverage and outstanding findings with the exact outputs. Packaging is not an approval decision. Publication, upload, purchases, messages and proof approval remain separate actions governed by the user's actual instruction.

## References and the stopping rule

At each material consultation, disclose the exact source and what it changed. Foundation guidance, story/cultural evidence, canon, supplied identity material, fonts, code, templates and artwork all have distinct provenance. Continued use of an established principle is not a new read. Without a vault, use supplied material and state that no vault references were used. Historical snapshots bundled with this skill are optional background, not required private inputs.

For each issue, identify its goal and actual gap, preserve protected qualities, repair the necessary scope, follow dependencies and inspect the result. Stop when the requested endpoint meets its established criteria, required coverage is complete, blocking issues are resolved and residual limits are explicit. A new version or additional pass needs a demonstrated reason.

## Skill maintenance and evidence

Run the [portable self-tests](portable-selftests.md), relevant actual-file integration cases and [independent scenarios](skill-evaluation.md) after consequential changes. Exercise successful output, meaningful refusals, interrupted recovery, stale evidence and prior-version preservation. Use actual rendered inspection for layout changes. Synthetic reviewer dispositions test reconciliation mechanics; they are never human acceptance of a book. A test barcode is not an assigned publication identifier.

Keep results outside the reusable package. Record exact tested versions and pass/fail/skip/not-run honestly. Standalone means the operational guidance, adapters and tests ship here; optional runtimes, permitted fonts, renderers and validators still need to be available for the corresponding capabilities. No original vault or prior skill is required to execute the supported workflow.
