# Synthetic cover-workflow example

Private conversations, artwork selections, identity details and live publishing records are omitted. This invented example illustrates the portable CD01–CD12 workflow and establishes no actual review, platform acceptance or user preference.

An author requests a paperback cover with a clear series identity and a story-grounded focal scene. Recover current direction, then establish fixed identity and permitted variation. Use stable chapter/appearance evidence to brief art, and test actual copy/type against a demanding background or crop when needed.

Give back copy, biography, spine and any flaps their intended roles. Preserve supplied identity facts. Choose icons from actual story evidence if requested; test apparent weight at the real spine size. Apply targeted type/contrast repairs and inspect affected areas in context.

Assemble each physical edition only when its relevant current interior, identifier and applicable template facts exist. Inspect actual exported panels and technical findings. Keep source images and selected derivatives; distinguish native pixels from reconstructed detail.

Match the exact interior-cover pair. Report the highest evidenced local or authorized external state. A progress bar, old summary or accepted cover alone does not identify the intended full release. Digital barcode checks, when supported by the selected version, remain separate from physical scanning and assignment.

See the complete [cover workflow](cover-design-workflow.md). Example scenes, decoration, dimensions, fonts, publishers and numeric targets are project choices, not universal rules.
